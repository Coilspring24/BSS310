### Section 2: Project Scope and Stakeholders

#### 2.1 Project Scope Statement

The scope of the Production Portfolio is confined to the physical realization, technical verification, and environmental hardening of the TerraNode hardware system within a decentralized manufacturing cell. 

To ensure clear boundaries for the production system, the scope is defined by the following parameters:

* **Specific Deliverables:** The complete manufacture, validation, and handover of 49 operational field nodes and one (1) central gateway.
* **Budget:** The production facility setup and execution are constrained to a capital set-up budget of R 26,000.00. 
* **Completion Date:** All nodes must pass technical validation and be handed over to the Logistics portfolio for field deployment by [Insert specific date, e.g., 20 May 2026].

**In-Scope (Production System Capabilities):**

* Establishment of assembly lines for Standard and Anchor nodes.
* Precision electronic integration of ESP32 MCUs, LoRa modules, and battery management components.
* Flashing validated firmware and conducting automated pre-assembly I/O integrity checks.
* Additive fabrication (3D printing) of internal structural supports and executing epoxy potting for IP67 environmental compliance.
* Final electrical verification using digital multimeters to confirm operational specifications.

**Out-of-Scope (Exclusions):**

* Topographical site surveying and physical field installation of the hardware (Logistics Portfolio).
* Execution of long-term market expansion and customer acquisition strategies (Marketing Portfolio).
