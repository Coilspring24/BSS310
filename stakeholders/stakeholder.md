## Updated Stakeholder Analysis Paragraph

The key stakeholders for the agtech company can be grouped into **shareholders/investors, financiers, customers, users, suppliers, employees, regulators/government, and competitors**. The **founders and Chanzo Capital** are important shareholder and investor stakeholders because they provide capital, strategic direction, and growth expectations. **Banks, lenders, and asset finance providers** are also important because they may fund equipment, operations, or expansion, and will be concerned with repayment ability and financial risk.

The company’s main customer stakeholders are **arable farmers, mixed farmers, greenhouse farmers, and irrigation-intensive farms**, as these groups are most likely to purchase moisture and CO₂ detection devices to improve productivity, manage resources, and support better farming decisions. The main users of the devices are **farm managers, irrigation managers, and agronomists**, who will influence adoption because they are directly involved in monitoring farm conditions and applying the data in practice.

The company also depends on **DigiKey and Mouser** as suppliers of electronic components, making supplier reliability, cost, lead times, and exchange rate exposure important considerations. Internally, the **management team, product development team, marketing team, logistics team, and finance and administration staff** are key employees because they support product development, sales, distribution, and financial control. Regulatory and government stakeholders include **ICASA, SARS, the Department of Agriculture, Land Reform and Rural Development, and local municipalities**, as they may affect compliance, imports, wireless communication approvals, taxation, and agricultural operations. Finally, competitors such as **Urban Cultivation International, Sense It Technologies Pty Ltd, and Seletech** should be monitored because they may affect pricing, product differentiation, customer acquisition, and market positioning.
